Warning signs to discuss with a clinician

Some changes should be discussed with a qualified clinician, especially if they are new, persistent, or unexplained. Examples used in this sample include a new lump, a sore that does not heal, persistent hoarseness, unexplained weight loss, and unusual bleeding. This sample does not say that any of these changes is cancer.

A warning sign is a reason to seek a clinical evaluation. It is not a diagnosis. Many non-cancer conditions can cause the same changes. Only a clinician who can examine the person and order appropriate tests can interpret them.

What this sample does not do

This sample does not prove cancer, rule cancer out, or recommend treatment, medication, or tests for a specific person. People with a personal health concern should speak with a qualified clinician. If a change is sudden or severe, seek urgent in-person care rather than rely on this prototype.
